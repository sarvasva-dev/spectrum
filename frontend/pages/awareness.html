The 3-Color Bin System

🌿
Green Bin

Wet & Organic Waste

Biodegradable organic matter that decomposes into rich compost or biofuel.

  • Kitchen Scraps: Vegetable and fruit peels
  • Food Leftovers: Cooked food, bread, rice
  • Organic Matter: Tea leaves, coffee grounds
  • Garden Waste: Fallen leaves, flowers, twigs
  • Eggshells & bones
💡 Tip: Never pack wet waste inside non-biodegradable polythene bags.
♻️
Blue Bin

Dry & Recyclable Waste

Clean and dry materials that can be remanufactured into new products.

  • Paper & Cardboard: Boxes, flyers, notebooks
  • Plastics: Bottles, containers, clean wraps
  • Metals: Beverage cans, foil, scrap metal
  • Glass: Jars, beverage bottles (unbroken)
  • Textiles: Old clean fabrics and rags
💡 Tip: Rinse food residue off plastic & metal containers before binning.
⚠️
Red / Black Bin

Domestic Hazardous & E-Waste

Toxic, chemical, or electronic items requiring specialized municipal handling.

  • E-Waste: Phones, chargers, wires, circuits
  • Batteries: Lithium-ion, alkaline, button cells
  • Chemicals: Paints, solvents, insecticides
  • Sanitary / Medical: Used bandages, expired meds
  • Lighting: CFL bulbs, fluorescent tubes
💡 Tip: Request a doorstep E-Waste pickup rather than tossing in regular bins.

Material Category Guidelines

🧴 Plastic Management

Single-use plastic takes 400+ years to degrade. Squeeze PET bottles, remove non-recyclable foil sleeves, and bundle thin plastic pouches together.

📦 Paper & Cardboard

Flatten cardboard boxes to save space in collection vehicles. Soiled pizza boxes or wax-coated paper cannot be recycled and must go to general waste.

🍾 Glass Disposal

Glass is 100% recyclable infinitely. Keep broken glass safely wrapped in thick paper to prevent injury to sanitation staff during handling.

🔩 Metals & Scrap

Aluminum cans, steel tins, and copper wires have high scrap recovery value. Crush aluminum beverage cans to optimize storage volume.

⚡ E-Waste & Gadgets

Electronics contain valuable precious metals as well as hazardous lead and mercury. Always schedule an authorized doorstep pickup via our portal.

☣️ Hazardous & Chemical Waste

Never pour paint, engine oil, or industrial pesticides down domestic sinks or drains. They poison groundwater and damage sewage processing plants.

Community Do's and Don'ts

✓

DO's for Responsible Living

  • Separate at source: Maintain separate wet and dry containers in your household or office.
  • Rinse before disposal: Quick-rinse plastic milk pouches, food trays, and jars.
  • Schedule bulk pickups: Use our portal to schedule pickups for old appliances and furniture.
  • Report overflowing bins: Help your municipality detect hotspots early before pests multiply.
  • Reuse cardboard & packaging: Support the circular economy by repurposing packing materials.
✕

DON'Ts to Avoid Fines & Hazards

  • Don't dump garbage on roads: Street littering is a punishable offense and clogs storm drains.
  • Don't mix batteries in regular trash: Heavy metals leak into soil and cause landfill fires.
  • Don't burn plastics or dry leaves: Releases carcinogens (dioxins and furans) into our breathable air.
  • Don't throw loose broken glass: Always wrap securely to protect sanitation workers.
  • Don't flush non-biodegradable wipes: Wipes and hygiene products cause severe sewer blockages.
