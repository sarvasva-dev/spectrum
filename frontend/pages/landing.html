STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

How CleanLoop Works

A transparent 4-stage lifecycle from initial citizen notification to confirmed resolution.

01

01 REPORT

Citizen reports a waste issue with location details, category, and optional photographic proof.

02

02 PRIORITIZE

The system categorizes and prioritizes the issue automatically based on hazard type and repeat hotspot frequency.

03

03 COLLECT

04

04 RESOLVE
